# Duets in Latent Space

**Performance + Creative Coding + Generative AI + Sound + Concept by Marlon Barrios Solano**

> *This is the unseeable space in which machine learning makes its meaning. Beyond that which we are incapable of visualizing is that which we are incapable of even understanding.*  
> — James Bridle

## About the Performance

**Duets in Latent Space** is a live collaboration between the artist—situated before a laptop—and machine learning models within their enigmatic latent space. Through various forms of input, whether movements, sounds, or digital interactions, the AI responds in real time, generating visual, auditory, or textual outputs.

Conceived as a **lecture-performance**, this piece integrates web applications programmed by the artist to explore memory, affect, and speculation. It combines vignettes, storytelling, interfaces, software, movement scores, and re-performances, weaving cybernetics, time travel, queer longing, recursion, and migration with algorithmic playfulness.

## Understanding Latent Space

Latent space, in machine learning, refers to a high-dimensional abstract space where data’s intrinsic features are compressed. It plays a crucial role in generative models like **Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)** and **transformers**, allowing AI to encode, manipulate, and generate novel content based on learned patterns.

By reversing hegemonic AI narratives and actively manipulating its inputs, **Duets in Latent Space** explores the generative potential of AI models, forging cognitive recombinations through evolving texts, images, and soundscapes within ethereal spaces of desire, memory, and hybrid materiality.

## Technologies Used

- **p5.js** for creative coding
- **Next.js** for rendering web-based applications
- **Machine Learning models** for gesture and body movement recognition
- **Large Language Models (LLMs)** for text generation
- **Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)** for image synthesis
